Fun with Numbers - Math for Kids

By Ms. Sarah July 06, 2026 14 Lessons 250 Students

What You'll Learn

  • Automate tasks on their computer by writing simple Python programs.
  • Write programs that can do text pattern recognition with "regular expressions".
  • Programmatically generate and update Excel spreadsheets.
  • Parse PDFs and Word documents.
  • Crawl web sites and pull information from online sources.
  • Write programs that send out email notifications.
  • Use Python's debugging tools to quickly figure out bugs in your code.
  • Programmatically control the mouse and keyboard to click and type for you.

A series of Videos from Kids EdTech give you a detailed tutorial to create fun math lessons for young learners. This course is a detailed and easy tutorial to get you all setup and going with the use of our interactive Math program.

The tutorial guides you through the configuration of fun activities, creation of lessons, quizzes, and finally guides you on how to boost up your child's progress with Premium Math Add-ons brought to you by our expert educators.

Additional items like the course layouts and featured images are also explained in detail throughout this comprehensive course.
